Theoretical Framework & Methodology

Comprehensive overview of mathematical formulations, multi-stage architecture, SHAP explainability, and counterfactual recourse.

Motivation & Identified Research Gaps

Prior educational data mining systems suffer from three fundamental limitations:

1. Narrow Feature Scope

Evaluating only cognitive metrics (CGPA, test marks) while neglecting non-cognitive drivers like attendance, soft skills, extracurricular engagement, and socio-economic support.

2. Late-Stage Horizon

Predicting only during final semesters (Sem 7–8) immediately prior to campus interviews, preventing institutions from deploying timely remediation in earlier semesters (Sem 1–4).

3. Single-Model Black-Box

Applying a single monolithic classifier across heterogeneous educational tasks with zero task specialization, transparent attribution, or actionable recourse.

Mathematical Formulations

Equation 1 — Multi-Modal Feature Space Formulation
x_i = [ x_i^{(acad)} \parallel x_i^{(socio)} \parallel x_i^{(behav)} ] \in \mathbb{R}^d

Each student profile concatenates intermediate entrance marks ($x_i^{(acad)}$), demographic/socio-economic status ($x_i^{(socio)}$), and behavioral/co-curricular participation ($x_i^{(behav)}$).

Equation 2 — Stage 1: Branch Recommendation (Random Forest)
\hat{y}_i^{(b)} = \arg \max_{c \in \{1..C\}} \frac{1}{B} \sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}( T_b(x_i) = c )

An ensemble of $B$ randomized decision trees processes mixed numerical and categorical early records, providing robustness to noise and intrinsic impurity-based feature rankings.

Equation 3 — Stage 2: Longitudinal Academic Performance (XGBoost + GB)
\mathcal{L}_{acad}^{(t)} = \sum_{i=1}^N l( y_i^{(a)}, \hat{y}_i^{(a, t-1)} + f_t(\tilde{x}_i) ) + \gamma J + \frac{\lambda}{2} \sum_{j=1}^J w_j^2

Conditioned on student profile and predicted branch $\tilde{x}_i = [x_i \parallel \hat{y}_i^{(b)}]$, gradient boosted trees minimize regularized objective to capture high-order non-linear interactions across semesters.

Equation 4 — Stage 3: Hybrid Campus Placement Prediction (XGBoost + SVM)
P_{hybrid}(y_i^{(p)} = 1 \mid z_i) = \alpha P_{XGB}(y_i^{(p)} = 1 \mid z_i) + (1 - \alpha) \sigma(A \cdot f_{SVM}(z_i) + B)

Augmented profile $z_i = [\tilde{x}_i \parallel \hat{y}_i^{(a)}]$ feeds a calibrated meta-learner blending XGBoost log-odds probability with Platt-scaled RBF-SVM margin probability ($\alpha = 0.55$).

Equation 5 — SHAP Feature Attribution Decomposition
f(x_i) = \phi_0 + \sum_{j=1}^d \phi_j(x_i), \quad \phi_j(x_i) = \sum_{S \subseteq F \setminus \{j\}} \frac{|S|!(|F|-|S|-1)!}{|F|!} [ f_{S \cup \{j\}}(x_i) - f_S(x_i) ]

Decomposes individual student placement forecasts into additive local attributions $\phi_j$ relative to cohort expectation $\phi_0 = 0.680$, revealing positive and negative drivers.

Equation 6 — Constrained Counterfactual Recourse Optimization
x_i^* = \arg \min_{x'} \left[ d_{MAD}(x_i, x') - \mu \cdot P_{hybrid}(y_i^{(p)} = 1 \mid x') \right] \quad \text{subject to} \quad x'_{F_{imm}} = x_{i, F_{imm}}

Feature space partitioned into immutable socio-demographics ($F_{imm}$) and mutable actionable features ($F_{mut}$). Guarantees recommendations never penalize socio-economic background, focusing strictly on attendance, tutoring, hackathons, and mock interview training.
